# Storefront home surface

## Scope and visitor mode

Persuade. Home page of the Puffer Jacket Women store. A shopper arrives from search or an AI assistant, sees every category, and reaches a puffer jacket within two taps.

## Audience, job, action, proof, constraints

- Audience: women shopping for warm outerwear and layers, comparing with premium brands.
- Job: find the right category and buy.
- Primary action: Shop puffer jackets. Secondary: Shop all categories.
- Proof: placeholder imagery and sample products until the user supplies real ones. No invented reviews, ratings, prices or claims.
- Constraints: Next.js, static export, deployable to the user's domain.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard, executed without irony. The arrangement a luxury outerwear shopper already expects, built to the bar of Moncler US, Mytheresa and Nordstrom. No quirk, no novelty: clarity and craft carry the brand.

OWN-WORLD: Ink #121212 on white #FFFFFF, warm stone surfaces #F5F3EF, hairlines #DDD9D2, one oxblood accent #6A1E2B for primary actions only. Display face Marcellus, UI face Figtree, small uppercase tracked labels. Generous whitespace.

STORY: The visitor understands at once that this is a premium women's outerwear store with every category in one place. They believe the store is trustworthy and easy to use. They open a puffer jacket, read fit, fill and care, and add it to the bag.

FIRST VIEWPORT: A two-column split on desktop. Left: display headline "Puffer jackets for women", one sentence, the primary oxblood action "Shop puffer jackets" and a secondary text action "Shop all categories". Right: a 4:5 placeholder frame. Below the split, a single row of category links (Long sleeve, Sleeveless, Short sleeve, Caps and hats, Accessories) starts within the first viewport at 1440 by 900.

FORM: Standing exit, the category standard, played straight. Seed key 1cc33a6f. Chosen by the user in words: check premium ranked US sites, then choose colour and font family for a premium, authentic look.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
